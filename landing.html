Signals are generated during our trading session from 2:30 PM to 8:45 PM WAT (West Africa Time, UTC+1). The Opening Range forms between 2:30–2:45 PM, and breakout signals can occur anytime after that until the session ends.

The Opening Range Breakout strategy identifies the first M15 candle of the trading session (2:30–2:45 PM WAT) and records its High and Low. We then wait for a subsequent M15 candle to close beyond these levels. A close above the OR High generates a BUY signal; a close below the OR Low generates a SELL signal. Only candle closes count — wick breakouts are ignored.

Every signal uses a disciplined 1:2 Risk:Reward ratio. This means the Take Profit target is always twice the distance of the Stop Loss. For example, if your risk (distance from Entry to Stop Loss) is $5, your Take Profit target will be $10 away from Entry.

If Take Profit is hit, the trade is marked as complete. We do not re-enter in the same direction for the remainder of that move. If Stop Loss is hit, we also do not instantly reverse. Instead, we continue monitoring until a confirmed candle closes beyond the opposite side of the Opening Range before generating a new signal.
