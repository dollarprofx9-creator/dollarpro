Process

How It Works

Simple, transparent, and automated signal generation

1

Opening Range Detection

Every trading day at 2:15 PM WAT, our system identifies the M15 candle that closes at 2:30 PM WAT. This candle establishes the Opening Range High and Low — the key levels for the entire session.

2

Confirmed Breakout Monitoring

After 2:30 PM WAT, we monitor every completed M15 candle. A BUY signal is generated only when a candle closes above the Opening Range High. A SELL signal is generated only when a candle closes below the Opening Range Low.

3

Automatic Signal Delivery

When a confirmed breakout occurs, the signal is instantly sent to our Telegram channel with Entry, Stop Loss, and Take Profit levels. The system continues monitoring for SL/TP hits and updates you automatically.

4

Trade Management

If Stop Loss is hit, we notify you and wait for the next opposite breakout. If Take Profit is reached, we celebrate the win and monitor for the next opportunity. No reversal trading — only confirmed breakouts.
