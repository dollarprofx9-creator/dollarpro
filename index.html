Process

How It Works

01

Opening Range Identification

Every trading day at 2:15 PM WAT, the system identifies the M15 candle that opens and closes at 2:30 PM WAT. The High and Low of this candle become the Opening Range.

02

Breakout Monitoring

After 2:30 PM WAT, the system monitors every completed M15 candle. A BUY signal generates when a candle closes above the Opening Range High. A SELL signal generates when a candle closes below the Opening Range Low.

03

Signal Delivery

Once a confirmed breakout is detected, the signal is instantly sent to our Telegram channel with Entry, Stop Loss, and Take Profit levels. The system continues monitoring for TP/SL hits throughout the session.
